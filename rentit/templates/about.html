About RentIt

RentIt is a simple vehicle rental platform built as a college project. It lets customers browse available cars, SUVs, bikes and vans, book them for a date range, and lets an admin manage the fleet and approve bookings.

Our Fleet

A mix of cars, SUVs, bikes and vans across multiple brands.

Simple Process

Sign up, pick a vehicle, choose your dates, done.

Admin Control

Every booking is reviewed and approved before it's confirmed.
